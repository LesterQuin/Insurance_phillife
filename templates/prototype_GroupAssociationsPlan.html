Eligibility

Any in good health and actively-at-work bona fide member of the Policyholder who is at least eighteen (18) years old and has not attained his 65th birth anniversary.

Individuals engaged in hazardous activities such as deep-sea diving, mountain climbing, and underground mining are not eligible for coverage.

Termination Age

  • GTLIP: Coverage terminates at age 65.
  • GADDR: Coverage terminates at age 65.
  • GAMERR: Coverage terminates at age 65.

Participation Requirements

  • 100% of all eligible individuals.
  • At least 100 individuals at policy inception and throughout the policy period.

Premium Requirement

Minimum of Php 10,000.00 annual premium (net of collection fee) shall be required to install the plan.

NEL (1 Unit)

No-evidence limit is Php 20,000.00 provided eligible individual has not attained his 55th birth anniversary.

Term of Coverage

Each eligible individual will be covered for a maximum of one year.

Other Terms

  • Unprovoked Murder and Assault (UMA) is not covered.
  • May be offered as GTLIP with ADD only, subject to the same terms and conditions.
  • Participation requirements remain the same regardless of the number of units purchased.
  • Benefits, Rates, and NEL will be adjusted accordingly based on the number of units purchased.
  • Groups are subject for review before proposal is released.
